Privacy Policy

Grob · Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Grob (“the App”), developed by Wirawit Rueopas (“we,” “us”), handles information when you use the App on iPhone or iPad.

Summary

Grob is a photo collage app. It does not require an account. Your collages and history are stored on your device unless you choose to save or share them. Purchases are handled by Apple and our payment partner RevenueCat.

Information we process

  • Photos and camera. If you grant permission, the App accesses photos you select and/or the camera to create collages. Images stay on your device unless you export, save to Photos, or share them yourself. We do not upload your photos to our servers.
  • Photo Library (add only). If you save a collage, the App may request permission to add the image to your Photo Library. We do not request full library read access for saving.
  • On-device app data. Drafts, history, preferences, and similar app data are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchases. If you buy Grob Premium, payment is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to verify entitlements (for example, whether Premium is unlocked). RevenueCat and Apple may process purchase identifiers, product IDs, and related transaction metadata under their own policies. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Diagnostics. Apple may collect crash and performance diagnostics if you have opted into sharing analytics with app developers in iOS Settings. We do not operate a separate analytics tracker in the App beyond what purchase verification requires.

What we do not do

  • We do not create user accounts or ask for your name, email, or password inside the App.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your photos for advertising.
  • We do not use App Tracking Transparency / cross-app tracking for advertising.

Third parties

  • Apple. App Store, In-App Purchase, and device OS permissions. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat. Subscription and purchase entitlement verification. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

Data retention

Content and settings you keep in the App remain on your device until you delete them or delete the App. Purchase records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to provide and restore purchases.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke camera and Photos permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete local app data by deleting the App or clearing its data via iOS Settings where available.
  • You can manage or request refunds for purchases through Apple’s App Store account tools.

International users

The App is distributed through the App Store. Purchase-related processing by Apple and RevenueCat may occur in the United States or other countries where they operate.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
